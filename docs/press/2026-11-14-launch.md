## ZA Mail: a free, open-source standby mail server for organisations that cannot afford to lose email when Microsoft 365 goes down

*Released after this week's global Microsoft 365 outage, ZA Mail keeps mail arriving and readable on servers in South Africa while Microsoft is unavailable.*

[2nth] today released ZA Mail, an open-source standby mail system for organisations that
run their email on Microsoft 365. ZA Mail runs alongside Microsoft 365 in a South African
data centre or on the organisation's own servers. When Microsoft's mail service is down,
staff sign in to ZA Mail and carry on: their recent mail is already there, and new mail
keeps arriving.

The release follows the Microsoft 365 outage that began on Thursday 12 November and
continued into Friday. Reports of Outlook problems on Downdetector peaked at almost
130,000 in the United States just after 1 p.m. Eastern time, which is 8 p.m. in South
Africa. Microsoft's incident, numbered EX1550543, listed Exchange Online, Teams and
SharePoint Online among the affected services, so many organisations lost email and
their usual fallback at the same moment. Microsoft said it had no evidence of malicious
activity. Organisations reported as affected included a United States federal appeals
court and New Zealand's immigration agency, which paused visa processing.

### How it works

ZA Mail does not replace Microsoft 365. Staff keep using Outlook every day.

- The organisation's mail is addressed to ZA Mail first. ZA Mail passes every message on
  to Microsoft 365 and keeps a second copy in its own mailboxes.
- If Microsoft 365 stops, ZA Mail keeps receiving. It holds Microsoft's copies for up to
  ten days and delivers them when Microsoft returns.
- During the outage, staff open ZA Mail in a browser or a phone mail app and read and
  send as usual. Nothing has to be switched over, and no DNS change is needed.
- Sign-in to ZA Mail is separate from Microsoft, because sign-in is often the part that fails.
- Afterwards, mail sent from ZA Mail during the outage is copied back into Microsoft 365.

### Free to use, host and extend

ZA Mail is licensed under Apache-2.0. Any organisation, IT provider or hosting company may
run it, change it and offer it as a service at no cost. It is built from established
open-source mail software: Postfix, Stalwart and Roundcube. It needs only ordinary Linux
servers, so it can be hosted in any South African data centre or on-premise, which keeps
the standby copy of the organisation's mail in the country.

### What is ready, and what is not

ZA Mail is an early release, and [2nth] is stating its limits plainly.

- Working today, and tested end to end in a local test environment: receiving mail for
  both systems, holding mail while the primary system is down, reading and sending from
  the standby, and copying outage-time mail back.
- Not yet proven: the set-up scripts for a live Microsoft 365 tenant and for production
  servers. They are written but have not been run against live systems.
- ZA Mail covers email. It does not replace Teams, SharePoint or OneDrive.

[2nth] is looking for [a small number of] pilot organisations and hosting partners in South
Africa to prove the production set-up, and welcomes contributions.

### Availability

ZA Mail is available now at **https://github.com/2nth-ai/zamail**, with the design
document, operating runbooks and a test environment that runs on a laptop.
