Microsoft went down. Your mail does not have to.

ZA Mail is a free, open-source standby mail server. It runs beside Microsoft 365 in a South African data centre or on your own servers, and keeps your organisation's mail arriving and readable when Microsoft is unavailable.

What happened on 12 and 13 November

Microsoft 365 had a global outage that began on Thursday and ran into Friday. Reports peaked just after 20:00 South African time, and Microsoft was still restoring service the next morning.

Email was not the only thing that stopped. Teams and SharePoint were on Microsoft's list too, so the usual plan of "message people another way" failed at the same time.

  • 130,000Almost this many reports of Outlook problems on Downdetector in the United States alone, at the peak just after 20:00 South African time on Thursday.
  • Not only emailMicrosoft's list of affected services included Exchange Online, Teams and SharePoint Online.
  • EX1550543Microsoft's incident number. Microsoft said it had no evidence of malicious activity and would publish a report on the cause.
  • Courts, visasA United States federal appeals court was affected, and New Zealand's immigration agency paused visa processing.

How ZA Mail keeps mail running

ZA Mail does not replace Microsoft 365. Your staff keep using Outlook every day. ZA Mail sits in front, keeps a second copy, and is ready the moment it is needed.

  1. On a normal day

    Mail for your organisation arrives at ZA Mail first. It passes every message to Microsoft 365 unchanged and keeps a copy in its own mailbox for that person.

  2. When Microsoft is down

    ZA Mail keeps receiving. Staff open ZA Mail in a browser or phone mail app, find their recent mail already there, and read and send as usual. Nothing is switched over.

  3. When Microsoft is back

    Mail that was held is delivered to Microsoft 365. Mail your staff sent from ZA Mail is copied into their Outlook Sent Items. Everyone goes back to Outlook.

Sign-in that does not depend on Microsoft

ZA Mail has its own passwords and second factor. When Microsoft's sign-in service is the part that fails, your staff can still get in.

Your mail stays in South Africa

Run it in any local data centre or on your own hardware. The standby copy of your organisation's mail never has to leave the country.

Nothing to switch at 8 p.m.

Mail is already flowing to ZA Mail before the outage starts. There is no DNS change to make and nothing to wait for.

It only ever adds

ZA Mail never deletes mail and never alters a message on its way to Microsoft. A half-working Microsoft 365 cannot damage the standby copy.

Built for organisations that cannot go quiet

Hospitals and clinics. Municipalities and emergency services. Law firms with court deadlines. Logistics, utilities and financial services. Anyone for whom a night without email is a night without operations.

It is also for the IT providers and hosting companies who look after them. ZA Mail gives each organisation its own separate instance, so you can host it for many clients without mixing their mail.

ZA Mail covers email. It does not replace Teams, SharePoint or OneDrive.

What is ready, stated plainly

ZA Mail is an early release. Here is exactly how far it has been proven.

Working and tested

  • Receiving mail for both systems
  • Holding mail while the primary is down, then delivering it once
  • Reading and sending from the standby, with signed outgoing mail
  • Copying outage-time mail back, with no duplicates
  • Outage detection that pauses background sync

Written, not yet proven live

  • Set-up scripts for a real Microsoft 365 tenant
  • Reading your staff list from Microsoft's directory
  • Deployment to production servers

Not built yet

  • Calendar and contact sync
  • Warming up sending addresses before an outage
  • Support for Google Workspace as the primary system

The tested items run end to end in a local test environment against a stand-in for Microsoft 365. We are looking for pilot organisations and hosting partners in South Africa to prove the rest. See where help is needed.

Free to use, host and extend

ZA Mail is licensed under Apache-2.0. Run it for your own organisation, change it, or offer it to your clients as a service. There is no fee and no licence key.

It is assembled from mail software with long track records: Postfix, Stalwart and Roundcube, all open source.
